Computational calculations for both the LS and HS isolated molecules of Fe[HB(tz)3]2 were performed by DFT calculations[ref1] as implemented in the Jaguar 9.1 suite[ref2] of ab initio quantum chemistry programs. The starting coordinates were extracted from the LS (CCDC 2149760) and HS (CCDC 2149761) single-crystal structures, respectively. Geometry optimizations to the stationary points were performed with the B3LYP[ref3] hybrid exchange and correlation functional including Grimme’s D3 dispersion correction in gas-phase.[ref4] The 6-31G** basis set[ref5] was used for all atoms except for iron, which was represented by the Los Alamos LACVP** basis set,[ref6] including effective core potentials. The energies of the optimized structures were reevaluated by single-point calculations on each optimized geometry using Dunning’s correlation consistent triple- z basis set cc-pVTZ(-f)[ref7] that includes a double set of polarization functions. Iron atom was represented by using a triple-z version of LACV3P**, where the exponent of the basis functions were decontracted to mimic the flexibility of the triple- z basis. Analytical vibrational frequencies within the harmonic oscillator approximation were computed with the B3LYP-D3/6-31G** level to confirm proper convergence to well-defined minima and saddle points on the potential energy surface.
